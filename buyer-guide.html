Discuss your brief ↗

01 / GETTING STARTED

Give your idea a useful starting point.

You do not need a finished design to begin. Share what you know, and identify the decisions you still need help with.

  • Product: mouth tape, nasal strips or the selected wellness patch range.
  • Format: preferred size, shape, material and pieces per pack.
  • Quantity: your initial order and whether you need standard supply or customization.
  • Market: destination country, sales channel and delivery postcode.
  • Brand: existing artwork, packaging references or a short description of your direction.

Use the live preview to communicate appearance. Its references identify an illustrative design, not a verified SKU or physical size.

Compare the product categories ↗

02 / FROM ARTWORK TO SAMPLE

Confirm the details you can see and feel.

Ask our team which artwork format and packaging dimensions are needed for your selected product before preparing the final files.

  • Review logo placement, print colors and the actual cut shape.
  • Confirm pack count, pouch or box format, and the wording you intend to use.
  • Evaluate the material and physical sample rather than relying on a screen color.
  • Agree the approved version before production begins.

The US$20 sample package contains up to 20 pieces, with worldwide shipping and import duties included. A 30-patch standard wellness trial is a separate quotation.

Request a sample selection ↗

03 / PRODUCT & MARKET

Request documents for the actual product.

Tell us which product you are considering and where it will be sold. We will confirm available documentation, the product scope and validity, including CE documentation where relevant.

A document for one product should not be assumed to cover the whole range. Review the intended packaging and market requirements with the appropriate adviser before sale. Proprietary formulation details are not published on this website.

Ask about documentation ↗

04 / BEFORE CONFIRMING

Put the agreed details in the quotation.

Confirm the product specification, quantity, packaging, approved artwork, price, payment arrangements and delivery schedule with your sales contact.

Stock products support prompt dispatch; the actual date is confirmed for your order. Custom timing depends on sampling and our production team’s schedule.
