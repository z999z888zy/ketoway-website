FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Plan your
next order.

Need details for a particular model? Send your brief directly to Daisy.

What is the minimum order quantity?+

Mouth tape starts at 20,000 pieces. Stock nasal strips start at 10,000 pieces, and logo-customized nasal strips at 20,000 pieces. Custom-shaped wellness patches start at 5,000 carrier sheets of 6 patches each (30,000 patches). Ask us about a standard-product trial order.

What does the US$20 sample package include?+

Up to 20 product pieces, with worldwide shipping and import duties included. Share your product choices and delivery address so our team can arrange the sample selection and dispatch.

Can I order 30 standard wellness patches?+

Yes. A standard wellness patch trial order can start at 30 patches. This is separate from the US$20 sample package, which contains no more than 20 pieces. Contact us for a 30-patch trial quotation.

How long will my order take?+

Stock products support prompt dispatch. Your sales contact confirms the actual dispatch date. Custom-order schedules depend on sampling progress and our production team’s schedule; timing is agreed for your order.

How can I pay?+

Alibaba.com online transactions and other payment methods confirmed by your sales representative are available. Payment terms are agreed in the quotation.

Which product documents are available?+

Tell us the selected product and destination market. Our team will confirm the relevant available documents, their product scope and validity. CE documentation is provided where applicable; no certification is assumed across the full range.
