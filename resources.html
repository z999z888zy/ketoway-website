BUYER FAQ

Start here.

What should I include in my inquiry?+

Product and model, quantity, target market, destination postcode, packaging requirements and whether you need samples.

What does the US$20 sample package include?+

Up to 20 pieces and up to 0.5 kg total shipment weight. Shipping and duties are included only for eligible destinations; postcode coverage is confirmed before payment.

Which countries are covered by the sample offer?+

Eligible addresses in the US, UK, Canada, Australia and major EU destinations served by Yun Express. Remote and higher-duty destinations require individual assessment.

Can I get a catalog or certificates?+

Download files are being prepared. Contact Daisy for the current product-specific information and document availability.

Does this website save my inquiry?+

No. The current form prepares your message for WhatsApp or your email app. You must send it there; this website does not store the inquiry.
