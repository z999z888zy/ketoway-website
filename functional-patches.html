BEFORE YOU ORDER

A few useful answers.

Can I choose my own packaging?+

Yes. Share your artwork and preferred pack format. Compatibility, pouch quantity and final pricing are confirmed in the quotation.

How long do samples and production take?+

Standard samples dispatch in 3–7 days. Custom samples including pouches take 15–20 days. Bulk production is estimated at 7–30 days. Shipping time is additional.

Which payment methods are available?+

T/T bank transfer and Alibaba Trade Assurance. Deposit and balance terms are confirmed with your quotation.

Can you provide product documentation?+

Ask for the documents relevant to the selected model and destination. Availability and applicability are checked during quotation.
