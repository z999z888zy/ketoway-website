Can you customize the product and pouch?+

Yes. Size, material, print and packaging options are discussed for your selected product. Final specifications are confirmed with your quotation.

What are the lead times?+

Standard samples dispatch in 3–7 days; custom samples including pouches take 15–20 days. Bulk production is estimated at 7–30 days, depending on the order and schedule. Transit time is additional.

How do I request a quotation?+

Send your product choice, quantity, customization needs and destination postcode to Daisy using WhatsApp or email.
