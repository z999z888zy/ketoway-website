What is the minimum order quantity?+

Mouth tape starts at 20,000 pieces. Stock nasal strips start at 10,000 pieces, and logo-customized nasal strips at 20,000 pieces. Custom-shaped wellness patches start at 5,000 carrier sheets of 6 patches each (30,000 patches). Ask us about a standard-product trial order.

What does the US$20 sample package include?+

Up to 20 product pieces, with worldwide shipping and import duties included. Share your product choices and delivery address so our team can arrange the sample selection and dispatch.

How long will my order take?+

Stock products support prompt dispatch. Your sales contact confirms the actual dispatch date. Custom-order schedules depend on sampling progress and our production team’s schedule; timing is agreed for your order.
